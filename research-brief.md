- What task is difficult, missing, broken, unclear, or explicitly requested?
- What evidence supports that conclusion?
- If it is only a proposed improvement, what must be confirmed first?
- What specific deliverable could help?
- What remains unknown or inaccessible?

Do not choose a profession for the user. They may offer frontend development, integrations, visual design, documentation, or another skill. Present the evidence and service idea so they can decide. No budget, hiring intent, skill match or need is assumed.

## Pitch idea

Hi [team] — I saw [specific dated announcement or product]. [Supported observation, or a clearly tentative improvement idea]. Would [one concrete deliverable] be useful for [specific user task]? If it fits your plans, I can share a short approach.

Do not claim prior audits, prototypes, experience, deliverables or results unless supplied and true. Avoid generic compliments and promises of growth. An empty opportunity is preferable to an invented problem.
